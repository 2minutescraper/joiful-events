A little about Joiful Events

Our story.

I’ve always had a passion for planning and bringing people together to create meaningful experiences.

That passion naturally led me to plan my own wedding. While planning my wedding, I often felt like my husband and I were just another number on a calendar or another invoice to be paid. At times, it felt like our vision, our priorities, and the little details that mattered most to us weren’t truly being heard. That experience stayed with me because I believe every meaningful celebration deserves to feel personal, and no one should ever feel like their special moment is just another event on someone’s schedule.

That experience inspired me to create Joiful Events.

I wanted to build something different! A business rooted in thoughtfulness, inclusivity, and genuine care for the people behind every celebration. I want every client to feel heard, valued, and involved throughout the planning process, knowing that their ideas matter and their vision is worth bringing to life.

At Joiful Events, my mission is to create beautiful, meaningful experiences that reflect each client’s unique story. I believe event planning is about more than coordinating vendors, managing timelines, and bringing beautiful details together. It’s about creating an experience where people feel welcomed, celebrated, and cared for from the first conversation to the final detail.

Regardless of how many events are on the calendar, every client deserves to feel like their celebration matters.

Welcome to Joiful Events! Where every detail is intentional, every story matters, and every event deserves to be celebrated.
